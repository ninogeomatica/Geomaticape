about=GeomaticaPE is a QGIS plugin developed by Geomatica Ambiental to automate the preprocessing and analysis of multispectral satellite imagery. It is designed for GIS professionals, researchers, and technicians who require efficient, reproducible workflows with analysis-ready outputs.

    Supported sensors:
    - Landsat 4/5 TM, 7 ETM+, 8/9 OLI-TIRS — Collection 2 Level 2
    - Sentinel-2 MSI — Level-1C and Level-2A
    - CBERS-04A WPM — Pansharpening at 2-meter resolution
    - Any multispectral sensor — for PCA analysis (Landsat, Sentinel, CBERS, PlanetScope, RapidEye, ASTER, MODIS...)

    Key features:
    - Radiometric correction: applies official scale factors to obtain true surface reflectance
    - Land Surface Temperature: converts Landsat thermal band to degrees Celsius (°C)
    - Brovey Pansharpening: panchromatic fusion of CBERS-04A imagery at 2m with AOI clipping
    - Principal Component Analysis (PCA / ACP): generates PCA1, PCA2, PCA3 raster + variance bar chart for any multispectral image (auto-detects bands and resolution)
    - Multiband output: generates GeoTIFF with internal band names automatically assigned
    - Automatic resampling: harmonizes spatial resolutions (10m, 20m, 60m) into a single image

    All tools are fully integrated into the QGIS Processing Panel, featuring a graphical interface, contextual help, and automatic cleanup of temporary files.

    Developed and maintained by:
    Geomatica Ambiental — www.geomatica.pe
