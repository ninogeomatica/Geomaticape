# Geomaticape
## Plugins profesional para ayudar profesionales en QGIS en SIG y Teledetección

GeomaticaPE is a QGIS plugin designed to automate the preprocessing of satellite imagery, specifically the scale factor correction for Landsat (4, 5, 7, 8 and 9) and Sentinel-2 data. The tool simplifies the conversion of digital numbers (DN) into surface reflectance and radiometric values using official scale factors, allowing users to generate corrected and ready-to-analyze imagery in a fast and reliable workflow.

The plugin also enables the creation of composite images and the automatic calculation of Land Surface Temperature (LST) from Landsat thermal bands, delivering results in degrees Celsius. This facilitates environmental monitoring, land cover analysis, and geospatial studies by providing consistent, standardized outputs directly within QGIS.

GeomaticaPE is designed for GIS professionals, researchers, and students who require efficient and automated satellite image preprocessing for remote sensing and spatial analysis.
